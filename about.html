A Little About Me

the reader behind the moonlight

Hello, Fellow Book Lover!

I'm so glad you stopped by my cozy corner of the internet.

Books have always been my quiet places, my safe harbors, and my greatest adventures. Whether under the covers with a flashlight or beside a lamp long after midnight, I've always found the best stories happen when the world is quiet and the moon is watching.

This space is where I share those stories — the ones that made me laugh, the ones that broke my heart, and the ones that changed how I see the world.

Thank you for reading along with me.
